## Generated artifact policy

Runtime databases, logs, Python bytecode, local environments, and session/lock metadata are
local operational state and are ignored going forward. They remain recoverable from the starting
commit and remain present in the current local checkout until migration is reconciled. They must
not be edited by hand or used as schema-migration fixtures without an integrity check.
